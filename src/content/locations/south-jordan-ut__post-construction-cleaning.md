## Why South Jordan Properties See Post-Construction Cleanup Needs

Much of South Jordan's housing stock was built within the last fifteen to twenty years, and new-construction neighborhoods throughout the city keep that volume steady. Utah's dry, low-humidity climate along the Wasatch Front is part of the problem: drywall dust and sawdust don't settle the way they would in a humid region, they stay airborne longer and resettle on every horizontal surface, vent, and windowsill days after the crew has left. Add in the fine clay-heavy soil common across the Salt Lake Valley, which tracks into entryways and garages during grading and landscaping work, and a "finished" construction site often still needs a genuine deep clean before it's move-in ready.

## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process

We start with a dry pass, vacuuming ductwork, light fixtures, and ledges with HEPA-filtered equipment so we're not just pushing fine dust back into the air. From there it's detail work: adhesive and paint overspray removed from windows and flooring, cabinet interiors wiped down, baseboards and trim cleaned edge to edge, and a final wet mop or floor finish pass depending on the material. Tile, LVP, and hardwood each need a different approach, and new construction in South Jordan tends to mix all three in a single home, so our crews adjust product and technique room by room rather than treating the whole house the same way. The goal is a true final clean, not a surface wipe-down that leaves dust resettling within a week.

## South Jordan HOA Coordination and Insurance

A good share of South Jordan's newer subdivisions are governed by homeowners associations with move-in or exterior-condition standards, which sometimes means driveways, sidewalks, and garage floors need to be free of construction residue before a certificate of occupancy or HOA sign-off is finalized. We factor that into scheduling when a client flags an HOA deadline. Post-construction cleaning is typically a homeowner or builder expense rather than an insurance claim, but when specialty cleaning follows a covered event, such as a renovation triggered by storm or water damage, we document the work so it can be submitted alongside that claim.

## Local note

Homes built during South Jordan's heaviest construction years often have HVAC systems that ran throughout the build, which means fine drywall dust gets pulled into the return air and coats the inside of ductwork, not just visible surfaces. We check and clean accessible vent covers and returns as part of the final clean, since skipping that step means dust recirculates through the house for weeks after everything looks spotless.
