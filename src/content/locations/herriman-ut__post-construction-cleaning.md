## Why Herriman Properties See Post-Construction Cleaning Issues

Herriman has been one of the fastest-growing cities along the Salt Lake Valley's west side for years, and that growth shows up as a steady mix of ground-up builds, basement finishes, and whole-house remodels. New construction here tends to sit on clay-heavy soil that kicks up a fine, chalky dust during grading and foundation work, and that dust works its way into HVAC returns, window tracks, and light fixtures long before drywall goes up. Add in the valley's dry, high-desert air, which holds static and lets sawdust and drywall particulate cling to surfaces instead of settling, and a standard broom-and-vacuum clean rarely gets a property to move-in condition. Framing crews and subcontractors also track material through unfinished homes for months, leaving compacted debris in corners, closets, and stairwells that needs more than a quick wipe-down.

## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process in Herriman

The work starts with a dry removal pass: HEPA vacuuming of all flooring, baseboards, and window sills to pull out loose drywall dust and grit before any wet cleaning begins, since wiping dust first just smears it into grout lines and new finishes. From there the crew works top to bottom through every room, detailing light fixtures, ceiling fans, cabinet interiors, and vent covers where construction dust settles and gets recirculated once the HVAC system runs. Windows and tracks get a separate pass to clear out stucco overspray and paint speckle common on new Herriman builds, and all hard flooring gets a final rinse and dry to remove the haze left by sealant and finishing compounds. Bathrooms and kitchens are detailed last, since grout, fixtures, and appliance interiors hold the most stubborn construction residue. For larger new-build projects, the team schedules a walkthrough with the builder or site supervisor before the crew leaves, so any missed punch-list items get caught before the final buyer or inspector sees the space.

## Herriman Insurance and HOA Coordination

Most post-construction cleaning in Herriman is paid directly by the builder, general contractor, or homeowner rather than run through a property insurance claim, since it's a finishing service rather than damage restoration. Where it does intersect with insurance is on remodel jobs following a covered loss, water damage, fire damage, or storm repair, where the final clean is the last step before the contractor hands the home back. In those cases the crew documents the completed clean with photos so it ties cleanly to the contractor's closeout paperwork. Many of Herriman's newer subdivisions also have HOA architectural review requirements before a home is occupied or listed, and a documented professional clean can help satisfy that final inspection step without delay.

## Local note

One thing crews notice working Herriman job sites regularly: homes built on the benches toward the Rocky Mountain foothills tend to collect more windblown dust during the drywall and paint phase than homes lower in the valley, simply because of exposure to wind coming off the mountainside. On those builds, we often add an extra HEPA pass on window tracks and exterior-facing sills, since standard wipe-downs leave a fine grit that shows up under afternoon light.
