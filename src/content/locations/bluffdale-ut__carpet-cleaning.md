## Why Bluffdale Carpets Wear Differently

Bluffdale sits in the southern end of Salt Lake County, where the valley floor is drier and dustier than neighborhoods closer to the lake. Summers run hot and dry, and that dust works its way into carpet fiber through open windows, garage doors, and foot traffic from yards and gravel driveways. The city's housing stock is also a mix: newer subdivisions with builder-grade carpet installed in the last decade, alongside older rural and equestrian properties where horse hair, hay dust, and barn residue get tracked indoors. Hard water from the valley aquifer compounds both situations, leaving mineral deposits in carpet backing that attract dirt and resist standard detergent-based cleaning.

## Our Carpet Cleaning Process for Bluffdale Homes

We start with a dry soil removal pass, because loose dust and grit act like sandpaper on fiber if you skip straight to wet extraction. Hot water extraction follows, pulled through a truck-mounted or portable unit depending on access, and we finish with a pH-balanced rinse so no soapy film is left behind to re-attract soil within days.
