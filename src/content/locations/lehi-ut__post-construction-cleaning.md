## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process in Lehi

We start with a dry pass, pulling dust out of returns, light fixtures, and window tracks before any wet cleaning begins, because wiping dust before vacuuming it just smears it into grout lines and baseboards. From there we work top to bottom: light fixtures and ceiling fans, then cabinets and countertops, then floors last. Window and glass work gets extra attention here, since many Lehi builds use larger window packages to take advantage of mountain views, and leftover glazing compound or overspray needs a different approach than standard glass cleaner. We finish with a detailed check of outlets, vents, and track lighting, the spots a general cleaning crew tends to miss on a tight closing deadline.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Post-Construction Cleaning

HEPA-filtered vacuums handle the fine drywall and stucco dust common on Lehi job sites without just recirculating it through the air. We use microfiber systems and pH-neutral cleaners on natural stone and engineered quartz, since many newer Lehi homes and commercial spaces use these surfaces and the wrong cleaner can etch or dull them permanently. For hard water staining on fixtures and glass, which shows up fast given the mineral content in the local water supply, we use a descaling approach rather than abrasive pads that can scratch new fixtures before the warranty period even starts.
