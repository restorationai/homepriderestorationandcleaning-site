## When is it a plumber instead, and when is it 911?

Call a plumber directly when the water source is still active and you can't find or reach the shutoff valve, since stopping the flow is the first priority before anyone can clean up. Most appliance leaks, dishwashers, washing machines, water heaters, ice makers, have a dedicated shutoff valve behind or under the unit. Turning that valve clockwise stops the water in seconds.

If you smell gas near a leaking water heater, or if water is anywhere near an electrical outlet or panel, skip the phone tree entirely and get people out of the house, then call 911 or your utility company. Electrical hazards and gas leaks are not a restoration-company problem until the hazard itself is cleared.

Once the water is off and the scene is safe, that's when a restoration crew earns its keep: pulling up saturated carpet pad, running air movers under cabinets, and checking moisture levels in the subfloor with a meter rather than guessing by feel.
