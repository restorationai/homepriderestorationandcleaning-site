## Who do I call first when a frozen pipe bursts in Saratoga Springs?

Call a plumber first for the pipe itself, then call a water damage restoration company for the standing water and wet materials. A frozen pipe burst is really two separate problems: a broken pipe that needs a plumbing fix, and water damage that needs extraction and structural drying.

## How do you know if a frozen pipe actually burst in a wall or under the house?

The clearest signs are a sudden drop in water pressure, a hissing or dripping sound inside a wall, and water stains or bulging drywall near an exterior wall or the garage ceiling. A musty smell or unexplained spike in your water bill can also point to a hidden leak.

In a crawl space or under a slab addition, look for damp insulation, pooling near the foundation vents, or a sagging subfloor you can feel when you walk across it. If you turned the water back on at the main and heard rushing or dripping with every fixture off, there's an active leak somewhere in the line, even if you can't see it yet. Don't wait to confirm it visually before acting. ## What happens when a pipe bursts under your house or in a crawl space?

Water from a burst pipe under the house spreads along the path of least resistance, soaking subfloor insulation, framing, and ductwork before it ever reaches a spot you can see. Because the space is hidden and usually unheated, the damage sits longer before anyone notices.

That extra time matters. Wet wood framing and fiberglass insulation in a crawl space stay damp far longer than an open room would, and mold can begin growing on wet materials within 24 to 48 hours according to the [EPA](https://www.epa.gov/mold/brief-guide-mold-moisture-and-your-home). Water also tends to travel toward the lowest point of the foundation, so damage in a crawl space often shows up as a stain or soft spot in a completely different room. This is the main reason a frozen pipe burst in a wall or crawl space needs a moisture inspection even if the visible damage looks minor, not just a mop and a fan.

## What happens when you call for frozen pipe cleanup?

On the first call, a restoration crew will ask what's wet, how long it's been standing, and whether the water is still running, so have the main valve location and a rough timeline ready. From there, expect a moisture assessment, extraction of standing water, and placement of air movers and dehumidifiers to dry the structure in place where possible.

Technicians use moisture meters and thermal imaging to find water that has traveled into walls, subfloor, or crawl space insulation, not just what's visible on the surface. Materials that can't be dried, like soaked insulation or swollen drywall, get removed to stop mold growth before it starts. Photos and moisture readings are documented throughout, which matters for the insurance step that comes next. ## Does homeowners insurance cover a frozen pipe burst in Utah?

Most homeowners policies cover sudden, accidental water damage from a frozen pipe, but coverage depends on whether the home was properly heated and maintained. Insurers can deny a claim if the home sat unheated and the policy required reasonable precautions against freezing.

Document the damage with photos before cleanup starts and keep every receipt.
